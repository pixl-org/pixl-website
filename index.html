Why We Exist

PIXL was founded on a simple truth: talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Too often, students feel like the "only one" in the room in technical spaces.

"On paper, my XR journey looked like a dream... But something always felt off. I was often the ‘only one’ in the room… PIXL exists so more people can feel included and capable to build."

— Ananya Srinivasan, Founder

What We've Done

Check out some of the amazing events and programs we've organized for our community

MARCH 2026

Worlds in Action Hackathon

We co-organized an incredible hackathon in San Francisco with ICXR and XR Bootcamp. The event featured 40+ robots on-site, PICO devkits, and judges from Google, Meta, ByteDance, and Niantic.

FELLOWSHIP

XR Design Fellowship

We partnered with XR Bootcamp to provide free XR Design Fellowships valued at $1,800 to our student ambassadors. Members got guidance from mentors at Meta, Microsoft, and Samsung.

WORKSHOPS

Virtual Workshops

We hosted free virtual workshops open to anyone, teaching Unity, Unreal Engine, WebXR, and NVIDIA Isaac. Hundreds of students learned to integrate world model workflows.

Worlds in Action Hackathon

PIXL was proud to be one of the main organizers of this incredible hackathon in San Francisco, in collaboration with ICXR and XR Bootcamp. Focusing on "world models" simulating real-world physics, this in-person event featured 40+ robots on-site, PICO devkits, and judges from Google, Meta, ByteDance, and Niantic.

We Attended GDC 2026!

PIXL made history at the Game Developers Conference in San Francisco

GDC 2026 Trip

We successfully organized a major trip to the Game Developers Conference. By securing $12,000 in scholarships, we brought 10 university women from across the nation to GDC, providing each of them with a free pass worth $1,200. This gave them the opportunity to engage directly with the global gaming industry and marked a massive milestone for our growing community!
